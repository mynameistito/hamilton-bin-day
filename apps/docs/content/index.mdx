This guide explains how to use Hamilton Bin Day and what each collection week means. The schedule is looked up from the public Hamilton City Council Fight the Landfill service.

## Find your collection

1. Open the [address lookup](/) and enter your Hamilton street address.
2. Choose a suggested address if your search needs refining.
3. Check the next collection date, week colour, and bins to put out.

## MCP endpoint

MCP-compatible clients can connect to `https://bin-day.mynameistito.com/api/mcp` using Streamable HTTP. The stateless, read-only `lookup_bin_schedule` tool accepts an `address` string (for example, `12 Grey Street`) and returns the matched address and schedule, or address suggestions when no match is found. Invalid addresses and Council API failures are returned as tool errors. Requests with a cross-origin `Origin` header are rejected. There is no local MCP server process or API key to configure; the endpoint uses the public Hamilton City Council lookup service.

Collection dates can change around public holidays or due to council service updates. Check the council's [official waste and recycling information](https://www.hamilton.govt.nz/) if you need to confirm a change.

## Red and yellow weeks

| Week        | Containers                                             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Red week    | Red rubbish bin and food scraps bin                    |
| Yellow week | Yellow recycling bin, glass crate, and food scraps bin |

Food scraps are collected every week. Set out the listed containers on your regular collection day, with lids closed and placed at the kerb according to council guidance.

## Data and privacy

Address searches are sent to Hamilton City Council's public lookup API. A submitted address appears in the page's `?query=` URL; after a successful lookup, it is remembered in your browser in a cookie (up to one year) or local storage (no site-set expiry). Cloudflare delivers the site and provides Web Analytics; Cloudflare Zaraz is configured for Google Analytics 4 page views and a Twitter/X Pixel for events. The site does not keep an address-search database, but its service providers may process request or analytics information. Read the full [privacy policy](/docs/privacy/) and [terms of service](/docs/terms/).

## About this project

Hamilton Bin Day is an unofficial community tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hamilton City Council. Schedules are provided as a convenience; council information is authoritative. Browse the [project source on GitHub](https://github.com/mynameistito/hcc-bin-day).
