## Where the data comes from

The website and CLI use Hamilton City Council's public Fight the Landfill lookup service. Schedules are provided as a convenience, are not controlled by this project, and may change. Check the [Council's official waste and recycling information](https://hamilton.govt.nz/) when you need to confirm a collection.

Hamilton Bin Day is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hamilton City Council. Visit the [main website](/), read the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) or [Terms of Service](/terms), or browse the [project source on GitHub](https://github.com/mynameistito/hamilton-bin-day).
