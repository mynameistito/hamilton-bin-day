Hamilton Bin Day helps you look up Hamilton City Council bin collections from the web, command line, or an AI client. These guides explain how to use each option and what to expect from the results.

## Choose how to use it

- [Website guide](/docs/website/): look up a home collection and understand the schedule.
- [CLI guide](/docs/cli/): install the command-line client and use JSON output in scripts.
- [MCP and AI integrations](/docs/mcp/): connect an MCP-compatible AI client to the schedule lookup tool.

## Where the data comes from

The website and CLI use Hamilton City Council's public Fight the Landfill lookup service. Schedules are provided as a convenience, are not controlled by this project, and may change. Check the [Council's official waste and recycling information](https://hamilton.govt.nz/) when you need to confirm a collection.

Hamilton Bin Day is an unofficial community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Hamilton City Council. Visit the [main website](/) or browse the [project source on GitHub](https://github.com/mynameistito/hcc-bin-day).

## Red and yellow weeks

| Week        | Containers                                             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Red week    | Red rubbish bin and food scraps bin                    |
| Yellow week | Yellow recycling bin, glass crate, and food scraps bin |

Food scraps are collected every week. Set out the listed containers on your regular collection day, with lids closed and placed at the kerb according to council guidance.

## Data and privacy

Address searches are sent to Hamilton City Council's public lookup API. A submitted address appears in the page's `?query=` URL; after a successful lookup, it is remembered in your browser in a cookie (up to one year) or local storage (no site-set expiry). Cloudflare delivers the site and provides Web Analytics; Cloudflare Zaraz is configured for Google Analytics 4 page views and a Twitter/X Pixel for events. The site does not keep an address-search database, but its service providers may process request or analytics information. Read the full [privacy policy](/docs/privacy/) and [terms of service](/docs/terms/).
